# Analytic Research Agenda: Riemann Zeta Guth-Maynard Proof

The project has a warning-free compiling algebraic, combinatorial, asymptotic, and initial classical-density infrastructure layer. Beta removal, local zero multiplicity, Montgomery mean value, the finite Möbius mollifier algebra, the pole-free Ingham detector, the detector-to-Jensen divisor bridge, divisor-count growth, ordered-factorization growth, and powered detector-coefficient growth are proved. No direct project axiom remains. The unproved work is confined to the named #15, #18, and #19 analytic outputs.

The remaining assumptions are analytic inputs requiring advanced integration with Mathlib's measure theory, complex analysis, and Fourier-analysis libraries. The arithmetic coefficient inputs formerly assigned to #17 are complete.

## Remaining Analytic Obligations

### 1. Guth–Maynard large-values architecture

- The two historical `Decoupling.lean` contracts were false, not merely unproved. The interval-free incidence statement had no scale parameter capable of controlling arbitrary finite separated sets. The block contract had an off-by-one counterexample at `K=N` with the coefficient supported at `N+1`. Guth–Maynard's paper does not use a parabola-decoupling theorem; the file, both contracts, and both project axioms have been deleted.
- Their replacement is the source chain: a smooth Dirichlet-polynomial sampling matrix, singular values and trace-cube reduction, Poisson expansion, the `S₁`/`S₂`/`S₃` estimates, the Section 6 smooth reflection formula, the Heath–Brown difference-set mean-square estimate, affine transformations, tolerance-one approximate additive energy, and the GCD/spacing assembly. Only that chain may discharge `GuthMaynardLargeValues`.

The current closeout queue, from least to greatest estimated difficulty, is:

1. **#15 actual-`X` Type-II powered MHH:** generalize the existing finite endpoint theorem from `X=1` to the dichotomy's real cutoff using #17 coefficient growth. This is the most accessible new interface.
2. **#15 medium full-source Poisson remainder:** use the proved exact retained-mode Mellin reflection, arbitrary-order Fourier integration by parts, and endpoint-block certificates to bound the zero, wrong-sign, and far-frequency modes uniformly below the witness threshold.
3. **#18 smooth `ζ²` AFE and diagonal:** close this independent node while keeping DFI separate.
4. **#15 Type-I range resolution:** consume the complete medium remainder and the already proved short/reflected deweighting identities with MHH/Weyl certificates.
5. **#19 Heath–Brown and `S₂`:** prove the native difference-set mean-square theorem and its consumer.

Completed closeout: **#19 smooth reflection.** `LargeValuesReflection.lean` proves the signed finite Lemma 6.2 formula, aggregate estimate, uniform two-parameter decay, `T₀⁻¹/²` reflection-integral bound, and explicit `T⁻¹⁰⁰` far-frequency control. The full #19 energy-moment node, #18 twisted fourth moment, `S₂`, refined `S₃`, and the final density theorems are not independent closeouts yet. They consume Heath–Brown, DFI, Proposition 9.1, or other open nodes. Those three source theorems remain the independent hard cores after the four-package queue.

## Primary Research Sources and Reusable Formal APIs

- [ANTEDB finite zero-density chapter](https://teorth.github.io/expdb/blueprint/zero-density-chapter.html): #15 mathematical blueprint and exponent assembly; its Python code is not an analytic Lean proof.
- [Kiral–Petrow–Young, *Oscillatory integrals with uniformity in parameters*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.00916): a robust human-level stationary-phase framework. The project now avoids needing its pointwise main-term theorem for retained modes by using exact Mellin factorization, but its integration-by-parts bookkeeping remains relevant to the noncritical tail.
- [Maynard–Pratt](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid%3Ab2cc1c2e-6cec-495a-ba6a-61ba08acc723): #18 Appendix C coverage and reduction.
- [Mathlib Mellin inversion](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/Analysis/MellinInversion.lean): the proved `mellinInv_mellin_eq` theorem used in #18 coverage.
- [Hughes–Young](https://arxiv.org/abs/0709.2345): #18 twisted fourth-moment range and off-diagonal architecture.
- [Bettin–Bui–Li–Radziwiłł](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.02539): a stronger `T^(1/4-ε)` alternative whose Watt/Kloosterman machinery makes it less suitable than Hughes–Young for this project.
- [Guth–Maynard v2, 7 April 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.20552v2): the pinned #19 source proof. Sections 3–12 use a sampling matrix, Gram-spectrum trace dispersion, Poisson summation, smooth reflection, affine transformations, and approximate additive energy; they do not use the deleted parabola-decoupling model.
- [Heath–Brown, *A large values estimate for Dirichlet polynomials*](https://londmathsoc.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1112/jlms/s2-20.1.8): source of the #19 difference-set mean-square estimate used in Guth–Maynard Section 6 and again in the energy argument. Its three-term conclusion must be formalized as a native theorem, not retained as a premise.
- [Mathlib Poisson summation](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Analysis/Fourier/PoissonSummation.html), [Schwartz Fourier theory](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Analysis/Distribution/SchwartzSpace/Fourier.html), [matrix trace](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/Trace.html), and [singular values](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/SingularValues.html): direct #19 infrastructure. The sampling matrix is represented through its positive Hermitian Gram operator. [Mathlib additive energy](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/Combinatorics/Additive/Energy.html) concerns exact equality; the project now connects it to the source tolerance-one real energy through a proved seven-bin floor bridge.
- [PNT+](https://github.com/AlexKontorovich/PrimeNumberTheoremAnd): reusable Mellin, zeta, Poisson, rectangle-contour, and first-order nonstationary-phase infrastructure. It does not supply the parameter-uniform repeated integration-by-parts theorem needed for the complete #15 Poisson tail, the `ζ²` approximate functional equation, or the quadratic-divisor theorem.
- [Carleson van der Corput](https://github.com/fpvandoorn/carleson/blob/master/Carleson/Classical/VanDerCorput.lean): potentially reusable nonstationary-tail estimates, but no replacement for the #15 reflection or #19 smooth functional equation.

No screened public Lean repository supplies a complete implementation of the remaining #15, #18, or #19 acceptance theorem. Completion status is determined only by the pinned project build and transitive dependency audit.
