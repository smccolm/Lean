Status: authorized. Post-GM programs are isolated from the frozen foundation;
their planning documents do not themselves establish new theorems.

The Guth-Maynard foundation is frozen at exact commit `2ace9e7c09a69fdcd1edae1ab6deb7cb3b4df1be` and published annotated tag `gm-foundation-freeze-v1.0.1`. That commit passed the canonical release verifier from a fresh short-path clone. Hosted GitHub runs ended with external exit `143` before artifact upload; they provide no hosted PASS and are treated as optional, inconclusive evidence rather than a foundation blocker.

All new work must remain above that boundary. Do not edit the frozen `RiemannZeta/` proof modules, their public contracts, or the GM release evidence merely to make a post-GM experiment work.

The completed [Prime Shell](PrimeShell/README.md) experiment tested whether the
exact Zeta23 prime-prime trace kernel could be controlled from the cumulative
binary-correlation information obtainable from Guth-Maynard almost-all short
intervals. Its rigorous verdict is retained in that directory; it is not an
active excuse to edit the GM foundation.

The next program is the [Gafni-Tao exceptional-interval formalization](GafniTao/README.md).
It aims to formalize the published zero-density-to-exceptional-set transfer,
including the fourth-moment zero-additive-energy refinement, and then consume
the frozen Guth-Maynard density theorem. Its current status is architecture and
research planning only.

Neither program is described as a path to the Riemann Hypothesis. Exceptional-
set or density-one results do not imply RH, and no such implication is claimed.
