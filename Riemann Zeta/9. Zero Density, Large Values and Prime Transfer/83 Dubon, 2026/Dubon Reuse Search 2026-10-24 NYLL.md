The exact unresolved chain is **DUB-17 Theorem 2.6 / DUB-18 Corollary 8.2 → residual association → original primitive-form Frobenius characteristic polynomial → separate determinant → nonzero pairing on the required Hecke component → actual Weil nondegeneracy → divisibility on auxiliary Kummer covers and evaluation transfer**. The quadratic Frobenius relation is already proved locally; it does not by itself establish the determinant. The original arithmetic Tate pairing, Hecke self-adjointness and cyclotomic covariance have passed scratch audits. Scalar cancellation identifies the multiplier only after a nonzero pairing value is supplied.

The route decision is to retain NYLL as a specific source for the missing auxiliary-cover divisibility proof and its exact nondegeneracy consumer. Reuse the native original-curve pairing, finite maps, arithmetic action, Tate module, and audited Kummer entry witnesses. Compare the required generic representability bridge with extending the existing analytic Abel comparison to these covers before committing to a wholesale geometry port. The existing Abel comparison is for the original modular curve and does not discharge the auxiliary-cover premise. No inference of divisibility from a theorem name or from the original-curve comparison is justified. Unrelated NYLL class-field, anabelian and etale-cohomology developments are outside this dependency task.
