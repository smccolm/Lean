# Minimal modular-form source provenance

Original sources and Apache 2.0 license are retained from [LeanModularForms](https://github.com/CBirkbeck/LeanModularForms/tree/7c41b9b1747d47298f76bdb51f07031087702198), commit `7c41b9b1747d47298f76bdb51f07031087702198` (15 June 2026). Exact hashes and byte counts are in [modular_reuse.json](../../Tools/modular_reuse.json).

The port covers actual Petersson integrals, definiteness, the finite-coset pairing, Gamma0 degeneracy maps and their exact sparse Fourier coefficients, plus orthogonality arguments. It uses the pinned Mathlib invariant upper-half-plane measure and adapts the final group to Gamma0. It does not import the upstream Hecke/newform package or its admitted general-GLn and main-lemma statements. The local oldspace retains all g(dz) with d*M dividing N from positive M<N, including ordinary inclusion d=1. The original `NewformsBasic.lean.txt` is retained for attribution and semantic comparison; its incomplete generator set is not adopted. The actual discriminant now supplies a kernel-checked witness that a span restricted to d>1 omits ordinary inclusion.

The original comment that the integral over the standard SL2 domain is always a fixed multiple of the higher-level Petersson pairing is not adopted. The local integral is used only for integrability and definiteness; the higher-level pairing uses the explicit sum over cosets. Source snapshots are non-executable `.txt` files. Port compilation and audit acceptance are recorded separately in the reproduction manifest.

The original `QExpansionSlash.lean.txt` is retained byte-for-byte with its hash in the same ledger. Only its finite root-of-unity and convergent-series averaging arguments are adapted in `HeckeAveraging`, generalized to every positive integer. The classical finite Hecke formula, primitive cusp-form type and coefficient self-twist deductions are separate local modules; no full upstream Hecke algebra or newform package is imported.

The selected rational-cusp transport proof from `HeckeModularForm.lean.txt` and finite Mobius/transition calculations from `HeckeT_p.lean.txt` are retained and hashed. Their local consumers prove the analytic cusp conditions at every index and slash invariance at prime indices directly for Gamma0, including primes dividing the level. Diamond operators, the full Hecke algebra and the upstream newform package remain outside this port.

The byte-for-byte `FourierHecke.lean.txt` snapshot supplies selected finite divisor reindexing and prime-power arithmetic. The local proof generalizes these to a completely multiplicative weight, including zero at primes dividing the level, and constructs actual cusp endomorphisms at all indices from the already proved prime operators. The local determinant-normalized slash formula is authoritative; conflicting upstream normalization comments are not adopted.

The retained `PSL2Action.lean.txt` supplies the projective integer action and open fundamental-domain proof. Abstract tiling/conjugation arguments are selected from the already retained PeterssonLevelN source. The local port reuses Mathlib’s invariant hyperbolic volume, and the new Gamma0 coset projection uses the proved inclusion of the center in Gamma0; no Gamma1 fiber multiplicity is assumed.

The original `AdjointTheory.lean.txt` is retained for the selected adjugate identities and integral Bezout representative. These are adapted independently of the full upstream adjoint package; the actual hyperbolic transport and Gamma0 finite-coset arguments are separate local consumers. Good-index Hecke self-adjointness is proved and audited in the 303-module checkpoint and retained in the 309-module checkpoint; bad-index newform eigenbehavior is now derived in the local PrimitiveFullEigen module, verified in the 352-module aggregate and sequential checkpoint.

The original `SL2Surjection.lean.txt` is retained byte-for-byte for its self-contained coprime integral lifting and full surjectivity of SL2 reduction. Its only imports are Mathlib modules; no upstream general-GLn development is imported. The local `SL2Reduction` port and the finite matrix quotient consumers are verified by the aggregate audit and sequential foundation/paper runners in the 316-module checkpoint.

The retained LevelRaise source also supplies the selected integral T-factorization in `LevelLowerFactorization`. Its proof uses explicit coprime shifts and actual matrices, without importing the upstream conductor/newform package.

Selected general-dimension sources are now retained byte-for-byte: [analytic coefficient order](DimGenCongLevelsAuxiliary.lean.txt), [finite Fourier jets](DimGenCongLevelsFiniteDimensional.lean.txt), [norm factors](DimGenCongLevelsNormReduction.lean.txt), [norm order transfer](DimGenCongLevelsNormTransfer.lean.txt) and [dimension assembly](DimensionFormulas.lean.txt). The local selection reuses Mathlib’s actual modular norm and level-one finite-dimensionality, and omits redundant separate negative/zero-weight branches. Source hashes and the original license are retained in the reuse ledger. These ports compile cleanly and are registered in the 352-module scope; their aggregate and sequential verification passed with zero Lean diagnostics.
