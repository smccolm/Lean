## Candidates and route decisions

**Diamonds and transposes.** TauCeti's `ModularSymbols.periodMap_diamondOpCusp` proves the actual period-map identity for a cusp form of weight `w+2`; `diamondSymbol` acts on integral modular symbols through a Gamma0 lift of a unit. The proof reduces the diamond double coset to one representative and invokes the general period/Hecke substitution identity. This is a useful pattern for the remaining original Gamma1 diamond comparison. It still needs an explicit bridge from these modular symbols to this project's native H1 and arithmetic Picard Tate operators, with the original coefficient action and transpose convention. It is not a finite-character descent theorem. [Period/Hecke proof](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/a722fde649de5a203c320408257b17b389be2f00/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/ModularSymbols/Period/Hecke.lean), [diamond construction](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/a722fde649de5a203c320408257b17b389be2f00/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/ModularSymbols/Hecke/Diamond.lean).

**Integral Hecke characters.** `heckeTDiamondCuspNewIntegralAlgebra_finite` uses the injective period pairing and integral modular-symbol operators to prove finiteness over Z, including diamonds, for weight at least two. Its character theorem deduces integrality of eigenvalues. `numberField_CoefficientField` constructs the coefficient number field. These overlap substantial existing local proofs; replacing the local native H1 development with this different object chain would add comparison obligations. Retain the short local integral-span route and use the diamond argument when its exact comparison is needed. These coefficient-field statements do not construct a Galois representation. [Integral algebra](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/a722fde649de5a203c320408257b17b389be2f00/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Newforms/Hecke/Integral.lean), [coefficient field](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/a722fde649de5a203c320408257b17b389be2f00/TauCeti/NumberTheory/ModularForms/Newforms/NumberField.lean).

**Arithmetic model.** `gammaOneRepresentableBy` identifies the Drinfeld Gamma1 moduli problem with the constructed fine affine `Y₁(N)` for `N≥4` when `N` is invertible on the base. The inspected comparison proves the naive/Drinfeld equivalence. It does not identify a compactification with the project's actual integral-ratio function field or give the required Picard/Galois/Frobenius comparison. Keep it as a candidate for that model bridge rather than treating representability as arithmetic association. [Exact comparison](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/blob/a722fde649de5a203c320408257b17b389be2f00/TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/ModularCurve/Comparison.lean).

**Unramified descent.** Mathlib's `Algebra.IsUnramifiedAt.of_liesOver` now uses a flat upper extension and localized faithfully flat ideal descent. The proof is more general than the former Dedekind/domain/torsion-free version. It may shorten a later local-model argument, after the actual model and flatness are available; neither input is discharged by importing this theorem. The inspected changes to sheaf modules, ramification ranks and modular forms supply no missing original association theorem. [Changed theorem](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/ee6c124192fb5146db438068b8df8d84d8da4cf0/Mathlib/NumberTheory/RamificationInertia/Unramified.lean).
