The required dependency is DUB-17 Theorem 2.6 / DUB-18 Corollary 8.2 → literal higher symmetric-power continuation → arithmetic automorphy → original all-weight characteristic-zero association → arithmetic realization of the original finite Γ₁ homogeneous-coefficient Hecke characters → original Picard/Tate Frobenius and inertia comparison. Local reuse review: `SourcePrimitiveWeightTwoGalois`, `SourceResidualGaloisRealization`, and `SourceParabolicArithmeticResidual` already supply the proved residual Γ₀ arithmetic realization with its local trace, determinant and inertia conditions. Extending the original native comparison to the required Γ₁ and all-precision setting must be assessed before porting NYLL's scheme-level dependency chain. The new native arithmetic-diamond scope already proves the original field/Picard/period/Tate comparison and Galois commutation, so NYLL is not needed to reprove those discharged obligations.

The separate [parabolic-Shapiro candidate](https://beta.prove2.me/theorems/96bbed5a-210d-4d60-8934-d83a912e2875) remains a complex-coefficient Shapiro statement, not a verified implementation of the finite-coefficient counit lifting required here. [Wiese's finite-field comparison](https://arxiv.org/html/math/0511115v3) has explicit coefficient and weight hypotheses; it does not by itself prove the original all-precision parabolic-transfer condition.

Current decision: retain NYLL as a concrete source candidate for the original-model specialization branch; no production dependency adoption or claimed gate closure. The next route review must compare its exact original-model requirements with the existing Γ₀ arithmetic implementation. Parabolic transfer preimages, exceptional primes, coefficient-field/Frobenius/inertia realization, characteristic-zero all-weight association and higher continuation remain unresolved. Acceptance remains 16/20; DUB-17–20 stay open.
