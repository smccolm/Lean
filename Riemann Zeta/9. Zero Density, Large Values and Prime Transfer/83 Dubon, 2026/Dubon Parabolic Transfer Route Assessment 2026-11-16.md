The following is a mathematical inference from that geometry, not a kernel-checked counterexample. On the rank-two integral cohomology lattices of these complex curves, restriction has determinant of absolute value five. Its trace has the same determinant magnitude, since trace composed with restriction is multiplication by five. Thus the trace is not onto after reduction modulo five. Passing between the curve groups and the SL2 groups only introduces the central factor two, which is invertible modulo five.
