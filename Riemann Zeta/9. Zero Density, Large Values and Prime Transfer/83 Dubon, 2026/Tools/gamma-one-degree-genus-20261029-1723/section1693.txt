### Actual closed original matrix trace algebra — 10 October 2026

Construct the closed original coefficient subalgebra generated by all actual matrix traces of a whole representation. Prove trace membership and closedness. Prove that continuous coefficient maps agreeing on those actual traces agree on the entire closed trace algebra, using the pinned algebra-adjoin and closure principles. Prove actual trace invariance under the existing whole strict-conjugacy relation and use it to derive equality on the closed trace algebra whenever the original coefficient changes are strictly conjugate.

Modular Galois association, local deformation conditions at exceptional primes, deformation Selmer inputs and patching remain open. Higher symmetric automorphy and continuation, unconditional Deligne, non-CM Sato-Tate and the final modular application remain open. DUB-01-16 remain accepted; DUB-17-20 remain OPEN.
