Take actual lifts in the original idempotent left ideal of the genuine residual column units. For each original left-ideal element, lift the coefficients of its actual residual column expansion and subtract the corresponding linear combination. The original reduction-kernel theorem puts the remainder in the coefficient maximal ideal times the ambient algebra; the proved idempotent transfer puts it in that maximal ideal times the same left ideal. Apply finite-module Nakayama over the original local coefficient ring to prove spanning.
