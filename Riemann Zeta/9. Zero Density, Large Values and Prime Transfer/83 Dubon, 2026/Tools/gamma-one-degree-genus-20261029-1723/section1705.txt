### Actual adic matrix sequence limits — 10 October 2026

Prove that an actual sequence in the original precomplete adic coefficient ring converges when its nth successive difference lies in the original nth ideal power. Construct its adic Cauchy compatibility and use original precompleteness to obtain a limit, then prove convergence with the genuine ideal-power neighborhood basis. Apply this coordinatewise to the actual matrix product topology.

Modular Galois association, local deformation conditions at exceptional primes, deformation Selmer inputs and patching remain open. Higher symmetric automorphy and continuation, unconditional Deligne, non-CM Sato-Tate and the final modular application remain open. DUB-01-16 remain accepted; DUB-17-20 remain OPEN.
