Form the genuine trace Gram matrix with entries in an actual unit-reflecting coefficient subalgebra. Its mapped determinant is the proved original Gram unit, so reflection gives a unit in the subalgebra itself. Invert that same matrix there. Evaluate original trace pairings against the entire basis expansion and solve the resulting row-vector equation; every actual coordinate belongs to the original subalgebra, and the original matrix lies in its span over that subalgebra.
