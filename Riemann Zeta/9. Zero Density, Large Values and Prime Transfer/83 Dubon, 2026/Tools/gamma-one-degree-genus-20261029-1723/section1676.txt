### Actual continuous gluing from strict common conjugacy — 10 October 2026

Given actual continuous original coefficient representations whose common reductions are strictly conjugate, lift the genuine common conjugator through the original surjective local coefficient homomorphism. Its original identity residue is preserved. Conjugate the entire second continuous representation by that actual lifted matrix. Prove equality of the two whole common reductions and apply the already proved unique continuous coefficient fiber-product gluing. The actual first projection is the original first representation; the second is strictly conjugate to the original second representation.

Modular Galois association, local deformation conditions at exceptional primes, deformation Selmer inputs and patching remain open. Higher symmetric automorphy and continuation, unconditional Deligne, non-CM Sato-Tate and the final modular application remain open. DUB-01-16 remain accepted; DUB-17-20 remain OPEN.
