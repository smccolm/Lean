### Actual cubic matrix iteration bounds — 10 October 2026

Define the genuine cubic iteration starting at the original matrix X. Apply the proved noncommutative polynomial identities and actual matrix ideal-product bounds to show that its idempotency error and each successive difference have entries in I^(2^n). Prove that every actual iterate remains in the same coefficient subalgebra as the seed.

Modular Galois association, local deformation conditions at exceptional primes, deformation Selmer inputs and patching remain open. Higher symmetric automorphy and continuation, unconditional Deligne, non-CM Sato-Tate and the final modular application remain open. DUB-01-16 remain accepted; DUB-17-20 remain OPEN.
