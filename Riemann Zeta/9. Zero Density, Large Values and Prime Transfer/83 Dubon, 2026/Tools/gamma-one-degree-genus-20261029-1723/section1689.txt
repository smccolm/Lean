### Actual genuine original class fiber product and canonical map — 10 October 2026

Form the actual compatible pairs of original strict deformation classes using the existing original coefficient-change maps to the original common coefficient algebra. Prove that both projections of every actual original fiber-product class have the same whole original common class, by evaluating the actual compatible coefficient pairs on every matrix entry of a genuine representative. Construct the canonical map to this genuine class fiber product and derive its injectivity from the proved actual original class-projection injectivity.
