### Actual original closed trace coefficient ring and residue — 10 October 2026

Apply the proved closed-subalgebra unit-reflection and locality theorems to the actual closed original trace algebra of the whole representation, using its already proved closedness. Construct its actual residue equivalence through the proved subalgebra residue map, and prove that its whole original reduction is precisely ambient reduction restricted to that same closed trace algebra.

Modular Galois association, local deformation conditions at exceptional primes, deformation Selmer inputs and patching remain open. Higher symmetric automorphy and continuation, unconditional Deligne, non-CM Sato-Tate and the final modular application remain open. DUB-01-16 remain accepted; DUB-17-20 remain OPEN.
