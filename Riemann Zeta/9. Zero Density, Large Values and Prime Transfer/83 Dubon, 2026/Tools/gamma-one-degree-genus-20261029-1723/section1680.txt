### Actual finite local coefficient maximal-adic topology — 10 October 2026

Apply the already proved nilpotent-adic discreteness and finite-ring completeness results to that same original ring. Thus its maximal-adic topology is discrete and it is genuinely separated and complete for its actual maximal ideal.
