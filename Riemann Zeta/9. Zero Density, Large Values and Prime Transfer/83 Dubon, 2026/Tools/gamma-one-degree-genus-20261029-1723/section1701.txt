### Actual original closed matrix basis span — 10 October 2026

Prove that the actual span of an original full matrix basis over a coefficient subalgebra consists exactly of matrices whose genuine original basis coordinates lie in that subalgebra. Use the proved full-matrix trace duality to express each genuine coordinate as the trace against an actual fixed matrix, and derive its continuity in the original coefficient topology. A closed coefficient subalgebra therefore gives a closed actual matrix-basis span by intersecting these genuine coordinate preimages.

Modular Galois association, local deformation conditions at exceptional primes, deformation Selmer inputs and patching remain open. Higher symmetric automorphy and continuation, unconditional Deligne, non-CM Sato-Tate and the final modular application remain open. DUB-01-16 remain accepted; DUB-17-20 remain OPEN.
