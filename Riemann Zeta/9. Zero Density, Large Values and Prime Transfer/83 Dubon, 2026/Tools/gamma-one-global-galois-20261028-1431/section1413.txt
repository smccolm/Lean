### Original finite quotients and symmetric-power parameter maps — 9 October 2026

Four further production modules prove that the actual kernel of an original continuous matrix representation over a discrete coefficient ring is open. For an original compact group, its actual kernel quotient is finite; the faithful quotient representation composes with the original quotient map to recover the original representation exactly. The concrete coordinate algebra of a finite group over a Noetherian coefficient ring is Noetherian by the pinned polynomial and quotient results. Consequently, each original continuous discrete representation has its exact evaluation from the actual Noetherian coordinate algebra of its own kernel quotient. Separately, evaluation of the actual symmetric power of the original universal rank-two representation constructs the genuine contravariant map on coordinate algebras. Its universal and original-evaluation identities are proved from the existing coefficient-change theorem. The actual symmetric power of a genuine first-order lift reduces correctly, preserves original strict conjugacy and equality of the original adjoint H1 classes, and retains continuity in the original topology.

The finite kernel quotient may vary with the original representation; no single finite quotient for all deformations is claimed. These finite-group coordinate algebras and genuine symmetric-power maps do not prove complete local profinite deformation or pseudodeformation representability, local p-adic Hodge conditions, Selmer finiteness or vanishing, Galois association, patching, higher automorphic lifts or analytic continuation. Unconditional Deligne, non-CM Sato–Tate and the final modular application remain open. DUB-01–16 remain accepted; DUB-17–20 remain OPEN.
