This is algebraic representability of framed matrix representations of an abstract group by a concrete, potentially infinitely generated coordinate algebra. It does not prove complete local or Noetherian deformation representability for profinite groups, continuity of the universal representation, pseudodeformation representability, or a quotient by change of basis. Galois association, local p-adic Hodge and Selmer inputs, patching, higher symmetric automorphic lifts and analytic continuation remain open, as do unconditional Deligne, non-CM Sato–Tate and the final modular application. DUB-01–16 remain accepted; DUB-17–20 remain OPEN.
