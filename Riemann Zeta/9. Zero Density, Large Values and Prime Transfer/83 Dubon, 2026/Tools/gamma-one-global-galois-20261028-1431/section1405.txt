### Original fixed determinant, continuous first cohomology and restriction — 9 October 2026

Four further production modules derive the exact first-order determinant from Mathlib’s actual determinant expansion. Every original invertible dual-number matrix factors into its strict infinitesimal matrix and the constant inclusion of its original reduction; its determinant has the original determinant as ordinary part and the exact logarithmic trace correction as epsilon part. The original lift has fixed determinant if and only if every original cocycle value has trace zero, giving an explicit equivalence of those fibers. Every original adjoint coboundary has trace zero. The actual continuous adjoint cocycles form a submodule, all actual coboundaries are proved continuous, and their quotient defines genuine continuous degree-one adjoint cohomology. Its equality relation agrees with equality of those classes in the original algebraic H1. Strict conjugacy of original continuous lifts is equivalent to equality in this continuous quotient, and every continuous class is represented by an actual continuous lift. Restriction along the original continuous group homomorphism preserves actual cocycles and coboundaries, descends to a linear map on the genuine continuous quotient and sends each original lift class to the class of its actual restricted representation.

The continuous cohomology construction is the actual degree-one adjoint cocycle quotient. No higher continuous cohomology, Galois-specific finiteness or Selmer vanishing, local p-adic Hodge condition, or universal deformation representability is inferred. Associated Galois representations, higher symmetric automorphic lifts and analytic continuation remain open, as do unconditional Deligne, non-CM Sato–Tate and the final modular application. DUB-01–16 remain accepted; DUB-17–20 remain OPEN.
