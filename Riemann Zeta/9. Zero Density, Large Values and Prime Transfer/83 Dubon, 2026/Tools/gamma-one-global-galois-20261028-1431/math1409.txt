Four further production modules construct the actual polynomial coordinate algebra of matrix representations of the original group: one variable for every original matrix entry, with the ideal generated by the literal identity and multiplication equations. These equations are proved in the quotient. Its actual coordinate matrices form a genuine group homomorphism and hence are invertible, giving the actual universal representation. Evaluation at an original representation kills the entire relation ideal and descends to an algebra map; base change of the universal representation recovers that entire original representation. Conversely, evaluating a representation obtained from an original algebra map recovers the whole original algebra map. These two proved inverse constructions give a genuine equivalence over every coefficient algebra, and the coordinate evaluation is natural under coefficient-algebra maps.
