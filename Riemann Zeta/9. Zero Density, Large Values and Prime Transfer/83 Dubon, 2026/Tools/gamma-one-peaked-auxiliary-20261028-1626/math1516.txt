Four further production modules construct the actual ideal-class map for the pinned number-field Selmer groups. The original field-unit representatives map to literal divided-multiplicity roots of their original principal fractional ideals. Injectivity of positive powers on fractional ideals proves that this is a homomorphism. Original nth-power representatives have principal roots, so their classes vanish and the map descends through the genuine surjective projection to the actual empty-set Selmer group. Its image consists of actual n-torsion ideal classes. Its kernel is proved to be exactly the image of original ordinary units: a principal ideal root gives a genuine field generator, and equality of principal ideals yields the original base-ring unit. The actual number-field ordinary-unit theorem makes that image finite, and the genuine class-number theorem makes the class quotient finite. The actual empty-set Selmer group is therefore finite. Finally the pinned valuation-kernel theorem, together with finitely many original places and finite ZMod coordinates, proves finiteness of the actual Selmer group for every number field, every finite S and every positive exponent.
