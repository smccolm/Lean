For the actual rational arithmetic Galois group and an original continuous matrix representation with discrete coefficients, derive both finiteness of the original residual quotient and finiteness of original prime characters on its exact kernel. The previously proved fixed residual quotient theorem then supplies a genuinely surjective profinite presentation on finitely many original generators, with all original relations in the actual kernel. No finite-relation theorem or modular Galois association is asserted.
