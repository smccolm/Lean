The groups proved finite here are the actual pinned Kummer Selmer groups of field units modulo powers with original valuation conditions. Their comparison with original continuous finitely ramified Galois characters, the ramification bridge, arithmetic Mazur finiteness, final framed deformation-functor assembly and fixed residual presentation specialization, Galois association, local p-adic Hodge and deformation Selmer conditions, patching and higher symmetric automorphy/continuation remain open.
