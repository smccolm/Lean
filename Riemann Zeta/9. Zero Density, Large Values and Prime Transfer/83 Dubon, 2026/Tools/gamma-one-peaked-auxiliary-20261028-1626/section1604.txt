### Actual original inertia in the actual rational arithmetic group — 9 October 2026

The genuine integer-ring inertia subgroup is defined inside the same original rational arithmetic Galois group used by the deformation constructions. Its closedness follows in the actual Krull topology. The proved whole-union inertia theorem shows that every original nonzero prime outside the exceptional integer has trivial inertia in this exact group, with its original rational scalar structure retained.

Modular Galois association, local deformation conditions, deformation Selmer inputs and patching remain open. Higher symmetric automorphy and continuation, unconditional Deligne, non-CM Sato-Tate and the final modular application remain open. DUB-01-16 remain accepted; DUB-17-20 remain OPEN.
