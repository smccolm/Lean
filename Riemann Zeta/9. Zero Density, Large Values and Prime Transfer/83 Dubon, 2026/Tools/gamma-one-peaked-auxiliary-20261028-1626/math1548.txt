One further production module proves unramifiedness at the generic prime of the original number-field integer ring by actual fraction-field localization and field separability. It then uses the original unramified locus to prove formal unramifiedness after inverting the actual exceptional element, from index one at every original finite prime where that element remains invertible. This uses literal ramification indices of the original integer rings and the actual away-localized ring; it does not assume the desired localized unramified algebra.
