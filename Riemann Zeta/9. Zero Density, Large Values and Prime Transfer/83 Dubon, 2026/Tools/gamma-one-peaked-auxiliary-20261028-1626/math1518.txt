Two further production modules use the genuine Hilbert-90 theorem for actual finite field extensions to construct an extension-field unit whose original Galois ratios equal each given base-unit-valued character. If the original character is killed by n, its radical has nth power in the original base field, by the actual Galois fixed-field theorem. Separately, the genuine equivalence of nth roots of unity proves that every original extension-field nth root is fixed when the base contains a primitive nth root. If two actual radicals have equal base-field classes modulo nth powers, their normalized ratio is such a root of unity. Their original Galois ratios, and hence their original characters, are equal. The comparison theorem does not require a finite extension; existence of the radicals is proved here only for finite Galois extensions.
