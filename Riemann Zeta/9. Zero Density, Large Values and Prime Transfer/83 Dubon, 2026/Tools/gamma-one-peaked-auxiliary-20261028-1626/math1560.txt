One further production module defines the actual family of original finite Galois intermediate fields satisfying the original primewise unramifiedness condition outside the original integer. Their literal supremum is the constructed extension. Every original stage lies in it, and the proved finite Galois compositum theorem gives directedness when the original integer is nonzero. Normality of the original field supremum, together with characteristic-zero separability, proves that this literal union is a genuine Galois extension. This does not yet prove its finite-subextension ramification property, stage nonemptiness or cyclotomic inclusion.
