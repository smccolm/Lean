Define the actual unramified local condition as the kernel of original continuous adjoint H1 restriction to the genuine inertia subgroup of the same arithmetic group. An original first-order lift class belongs exactly when its original cocycle on inertia is an actual adjoint coboundary. Proved trivial original inertia outside the exceptional integer makes this kernel the entire original H1 there. The proved original finite-field arithmetic H1 finiteness gives finite dimension of this actual local-condition space.
