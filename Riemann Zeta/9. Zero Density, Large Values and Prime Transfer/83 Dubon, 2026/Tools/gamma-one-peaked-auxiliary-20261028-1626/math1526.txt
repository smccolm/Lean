Two further production modules prove that the ratio of any two original radicals with identical original Galois character ratios is fixed by the entire original Galois group. The genuine fixed-field theorem therefore places that ratio in the original base field; taking its nth power proves equality of the actual classes modulo nth powers. Thus every actual radical realization gives the same constructed continuous Kummer class, without a roots-of-unity hypothesis for this direction. Independently, the actual order of the image of each group element divides both source and target cardinalities. Coprime cardinalities force every original homomorphism to be trivial. The quotient of the ratio of two commutative-valued characters then proves that restriction to an actual normal subgroup is injective when the original quotient cardinality is coprime to the actual target cardinality. The genuine continuous subgroup inclusion transfers finite continuous character sets back to the original group.
