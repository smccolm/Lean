Two further production modules use original fractional ideals, prime multiplicities, principal ideals and the pinned Selmer group. Proved equality from prime multiplicities gives injectivity of every positive power on nonzero fractional ideals. The literal product of original prime ideals with divided multiplicities is nonzero and is an actual nth root whenever all original multiplicities are divisible by n. Its bundled unit version retains the exact power identity. The original principal-ideal multiplicity is identified with the negative integral logarithm of the pinned multiplicative valuation. The original valuation modulo n vanishes exactly when that integral valuation is divisible by n. Consequently, membership of each genuine original representative in the actual empty-set Selmer group is exactly divisibility of every principal-ideal multiplicity. This supplies actual ideal roots for the original Selmer representatives; no Selmer finiteness assumption is introduced.
