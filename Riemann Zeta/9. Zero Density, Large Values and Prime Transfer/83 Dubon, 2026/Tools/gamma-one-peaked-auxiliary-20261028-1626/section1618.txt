### Actual trace-free representatives of original adjoint coboundaries — 9 October 2026

When the actual matrix dimension is nonzero in the original coefficient field, subtract its genuine scalar trace part from an original matrix. The resulting actual matrix has trace zero, and its entire original adjoint coboundary is unchanged at every group element. Thus every original adjoint coboundary has a trace-zero matrix representative under this explicit characteristic condition. No comparison of continuous H1 with trace-zero coefficient cohomology is assumed.

Modular Galois association, local deformation conditions at exceptional primes, deformation Selmer inputs and patching remain open. Higher symmetric automorphy and continuation, unconditional Deligne, non-CM Sato-Tate and the final modular application remain open. DUB-01-16 remain accepted; DUB-17-20 remain OPEN.
