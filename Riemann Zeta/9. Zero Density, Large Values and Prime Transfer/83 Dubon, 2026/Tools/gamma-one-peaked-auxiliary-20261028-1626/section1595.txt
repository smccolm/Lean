### Actual the original dual-number product and adic topology identity — 9 October 2026

For a discrete original coefficient field, the actual dual-number maximal-adic topology is proved equal to its original product topology. The actual square-zero maximal ideal makes the first topology discrete, and discreteness of the original field makes the genuine product topology discrete. Continuity of the actual dual-number general linear group representations is therefore unchanged between those original topologies.
