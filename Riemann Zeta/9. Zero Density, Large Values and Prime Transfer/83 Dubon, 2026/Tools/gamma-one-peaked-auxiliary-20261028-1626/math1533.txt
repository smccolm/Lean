Two further production modules prove finiteness of the actual prime fibers of contraction in integral torsion-free Dedekind extensions. Each fiber embeds into the actual finite set of primes over the original height-one prime. A finite union then proves that the full set of actual primes above any original finite exceptional set is finite. The genuine ramification tower formula also proves that a total ramification index of one forces both original indices in the tower to be one. These results supply the exceptional-set and index steps for the pending transfer across finite number-field bases.
