### Actual continuous profinite presentation universality — 9 October 2026

Four further production modules prove the coefficient universal property for an actual surjective profinite presentation. The genuine completed coefficient evaluation of an original continuous lift is uniformly continuous, preserves the original residue point and agrees with that lift on every original abstract-group element. Density and Hausdorffness extend this identity to every original profinite matrix. If the lift kills the actual relation subgroup, the coefficient map factors through the literal ideal of all relation entries; the factor is continuous, residue-preserving and unique. The original presentation quotient is identified with the whole original presented group by the genuine kernel-quotient isomorphism, producing its actual universal matrix representation. Continuity follows from the true compact surjective presentation map. Finally, every original continuous lift with the specified residual values is obtained from that representation by exactly one coefficient map from the actual completed all-relation quotient; the theorem includes continuity and equality with the original residual evaluation. No abstract finite generation of the presented profinite group and no finite relation family is assumed.

The given presentation starts from the profinite completion of a genuinely finitely generated abstract group. Original arithmetic character finiteness, final framed deformation-functor assembly and specialization to the actual fixed residual presentation, Galois association, local conditions, Selmer inputs, patching and higher symmetric automorphy/continuation remain open.
