Two further production modules retain the actual coefficient algebra structure on the residue map of an original local algebra homomorphism. Surjectivity on the original coefficients gives surjectivity on the original residue fields; field injectivity then constructs their genuine coefficient-algebra equivalence, with the exact original residue formula. The actual coefficient quotient is a specific application, using its proved surjective local quotient map. For an original compact Hausdorff Noetherian local ring with its genuine maximal-adic topology, the original quotient maximal ideal is identified with the image of the original maximal ideal. The already proved quotient-completeness theorem therefore gives completeness and separation for the quotient own maximal ideal.
