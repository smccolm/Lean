## Adaptation and verification boundaries

The Dickson port retains Duxing Yang's Apache-2.0 notices and source provenance. It uses the native `Mathlib.Data.Matrix.Action` module, adapts renamed APIs, removes all upstream file-level options and per-declaration `nolint` attributes, removes four unused finiteness hypotheses from helper definitions, and confines a finite enumeration instance to the proof scopes that need it. No theorem's classification alternatives, prime range or group-isomorphism conclusions were weakened. Initial compatibility passes were not accepted as linter passes; the failed full audit and corrected successor are preserved separately.

The generic residual-image bridge constructs the entrywise coefficient map and projective range and proves their finiteness before applying Dickson. The next original-form consumer uses the actual integral/residual arithmetic action returned by `exists_gammaOne_original_fixedDeterminant_residual`; it supplies no representation or large-image hypothesis. Both the expanded conclusion and its equivalent named-predicate form hit the default elaboration limit. Both failures are preserved. A fresh decomposition separates the generic coefficient-map/classification witness from the original-form assembly, without raising limits or changing the conclusion. This original-form consumer and its final dependency audit remain pending.

The source chain still requires arithmetic irreducibility and exclusion of the unwanted projective cases, the correct subfield and matrix-image comparison, remaining local/regularity hypotheses, automorphy and literal higher symmetric-power continuation. The reviewed explicit large-image literature uses Deligne in a dihedral bound, so that branch cannot be cited circularly as a route to the still-open Deligne input. Abstract PSL₂/PGL₂ isomorphism is weaker than a full SL₂ image over the specific residue field required by existing consumers.

## Search scope and evidence

The November 17 searches targeted Lean proofs of residual Galois large image, Dickson classification, Deligne/Ramanujan–Petersson, modular Sato–Tate, and symmetric-power automorphy. Primary repository sources and exact theorem signatures take precedence over search summaries or announcements. No complete directly reusable missing Sato–Tate or higher symmetric-power endpoint was verified in the inspected results; that is a bounded finding, not a claim that none exists anywhere.
