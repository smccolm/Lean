### Actual original continuous adjoint coboundary dimension — 10 October 2026

The kernel of the original continuous matrix adjoint coboundary map is proved equal to the actual invariant submodule of that same original adjoint representation. Rank-nullity on the genuine matrix coefficient space gives coboundary dimension plus original invariant dimension equal to the square of the original matrix size.

Modular Galois association, local deformation conditions at exceptional primes, deformation Selmer inputs and patching remain open. Higher symmetric automorphy and continuation, unconditional Deligne, non-CM Sato-Tate and the final modular application remain open. DUB-01-16 remain accepted; DUB-17-20 remain OPEN.
