Explicit absolute irreducibility of the true residual representation of that reduction makes each actual reduced stabilizer a genuine coefficient scalar unit by the proved local centralizer theorem. The pinned surjective-local-hom units theorem lifts that actual coefficient unit. Its original scalar matrix stabilizes the whole original representation and reduces to the exact supplied reduced stabilizer. Thus actual stabilizers lift along the original coefficient surjection.
