### Actual continuous trace-zero cohomology restriction — 10 October 2026

Actual continuous trace-zero cocycles restrict along the original continuous group homomorphism. The same original trace-zero matrix gives the restricted coboundary, so restriction descends to genuine continuous H1. It commutes with the original full-adjoint inclusion. When the original matrix dimension is nonzero in the coefficient field, an actual trace-zero class restricts to zero exactly when its included original adjoint class does.

Modular Galois association, local deformation conditions at exceptional primes, deformation Selmer inputs and patching remain open. Higher symmetric automorphy and continuation, unconditional Deligne, non-CM Sato-Tate and the final modular application remain open. DUB-01-16 remain accepted; DUB-17-20 remain OPEN.
