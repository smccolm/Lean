### Actual arithmetic trace-zero inertia classes — 10 October 2026

The actual unramified condition is defined as the kernel of genuine trace-zero H1 restriction to the original arithmetic inertia subgroup. Under the explicit nonzero-dimension condition, the coefficient inclusion gives a linear equivalence with the previously proved intersection of the original determinant trace kernel and inertia restriction kernel. Its original full-adjoint class is retained. Thus dimensions agree, and proved arithmetic cohomology finiteness gives finite-dimensionality of this actual trace-zero local condition.
