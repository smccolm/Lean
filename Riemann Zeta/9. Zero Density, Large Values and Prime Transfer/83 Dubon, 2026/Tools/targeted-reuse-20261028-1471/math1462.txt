Four further production modules prove literal coefficient-ideal multiplication and power membership for the original finite matrix ring. The pinned commuting binomial formula shows that taking the residue-prime power improves the actual congruence order by one; iteration and genuine ideal nilpotence force a true prime-power order. The kernel of the original GL matrix reduction map is used directly: every kernel matrix differs from one by entries in the original ideal, and the proved matrix-power result makes this actual kernel a p-group. For an original Artinian local coefficient ring, Hopkins–Levitzki and the pinned maximal-ideal theorem supply nilpotence, while its actual residue characteristic supplies membership of the prime in that same maximal ideal. Thus the literal residual matrix kernel is proved a p-group, without assuming a kernel certificate or replacing matrices by a model group.
