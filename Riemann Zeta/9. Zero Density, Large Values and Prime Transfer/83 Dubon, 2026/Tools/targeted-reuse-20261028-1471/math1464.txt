Two further production modules prove that the image of an original ideal in each of its actual power quotients is nilpotent with the original exponent. The original coefficient prime remains in that image ideal; the proved matrix congruence theorem therefore gives a genuine p-group as the literal reduction kernel over each original power quotient. For the actual completed residual representation ring, its genuine completed residual evaluation proves that the original residue characteristic belongs to its true maximal ideal. The generic result then applies to these original maximal-power quotients. The already proved finite-quotient theorem makes every such actual matrix kernel finite when the original residue field is finite.
