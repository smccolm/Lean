## Native reuse before further expansion

The repository already proves actual Fricke torsion-function separation,
transformation, cyclotomic Laurent expansions, finite level fields and
monic-j integrality (`SourceFrickeSeparation`, `SourceFrickeLevelFieldV3`,
`SourceFrickeLaurentGeneratorIntegrality`,

Monic-j integrality alone is not p-adic Gauss integrality. The existing
diamond rational-generator and cyclotomic-lift results prove rationality,
not preservation of the coefficient valuation. No such implication is claimed.
The adjacent-j correspondence automorphism itself need not be Gauss-isometric.
