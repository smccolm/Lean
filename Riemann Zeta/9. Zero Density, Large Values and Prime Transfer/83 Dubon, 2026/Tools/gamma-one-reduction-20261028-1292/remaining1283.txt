The original fully split restricted tensor, individual finite local irreducibility/admissibility, full-real signed Hilbert basis and full orthogonal compact-type admissibility at every open finite adelic level are proved. The exact original local spherical matrix-coefficient, Satake-trace and Fourier prime-power formulas, and generic unit spherical-function/orbit-Gram uniqueness from the genuine Hecke eigenvalue are also proved. The completed equivariant spherical representation classification and Euler-factor bridge, automorphic non-CM bridge and higher symmetric automorphy/continuation remain open. Unconditional Deligne, non-CM Sato–Tate and the final modular application remain open. DUB-01–16 remain accepted; DUB-17–20 remain OPEN.
