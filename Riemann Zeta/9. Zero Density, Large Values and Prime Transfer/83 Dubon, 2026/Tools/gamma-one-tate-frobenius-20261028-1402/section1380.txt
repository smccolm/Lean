### Original arithmetic symmetric-power coefficient change and continuity — 8 October 2026

The existing homogeneous matrix substitution and universal coefficient-polynomial modules are generalized from complex coefficients to commutative coefficient rings, retaining their original complex specializations. The genuine homogeneous coefficient map is constructed semilinearly from the original ring homomorphism and proved to intertwine literal matrix substitution and the actual general-linear action. The original monomial basis supplies an actual homomorphism GL(2,R) to GL(n+1,R). Its coordinates are the original homogeneous polynomial coefficients. This genuine symmetric-power matrix homomorphism commutes with coefficient change, including residue-field reduction, and every matrix entry is evaluation of its original universal coefficient polynomial. Both its matrix and inverse coordinates are proved continuous over the original topological coefficient ring. Thus original continuous rank-two matrix representations have genuine continuous symmetric powers, with exact coefficient reduction and original changes of basis preserved. These are proved arithmetic functoriality foundations; no Galois representation attached to the original cusp form or automorphy lifting theorem is assumed or claimed.

The actual original good-prime induced subquotient and symmetric Euler normalization remain proved. The new coefficient-ring and continuity results prepare the arithmetic representation operations needed by the remaining lifting argument. Constructing associated Galois representations and proving the required higher symmetric automorphy and analytic continuation remain open, as do unconditional Deligne, non-CM Sato–Tate and the final modular application. DUB-01–16 remain accepted; DUB-17–20 remain OPEN.
