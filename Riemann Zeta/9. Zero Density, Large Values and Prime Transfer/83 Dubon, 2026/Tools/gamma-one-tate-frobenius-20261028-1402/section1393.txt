### Original natural determinant law, basis independence and direct coefficient continuity — 8 October 2026

Four further production modules construct the genuine natural multiplicative homogeneous determinant-law structure from each original matrix representation. The coefficient algebras range over an arbitrary independent universe; each law field is proved from the actual matrix determinant family. The original intertwining change of basis conjugates the genuine action on every group-algebra element, so it preserves the entire determinant family over every coefficient extension. The constructed law is therefore exactly invariant under original changes of basis and commutes with actual coefficient reduction. For every finite tuple, the law evaluated on the original group-algebra linear combination is exactly the determinant polynomial of the corresponding original group matrices. Each multivariable coefficient of this polynomial is itself the evaluation of an explicit universal polynomial in all the original tuple entries. These identities directly prove continuity of every such coefficient for every original continuous representation, without assuming a general characteristic-coefficient reconstruction theorem.

These results construct the representation-to-determinant family and its original-object identities. They do not construct the Galois representation associated to the original cusp form, represent a pseudodeformation functor, or prove automorphy lifting. Higher symmetric automorphic lifts and analytic continuation remain open, as do unconditional Deligne, non-CM Sato-Tate and the final modular application. DUB-01–16 remain accepted; DUB-17–20 remain OPEN.
